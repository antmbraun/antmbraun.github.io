Clay privacy policy

Clay is a personal tool used only by its author, on the author's own computer.

When the author signs in with Google, Clay reads the author's Gmail messages to search them and send the author notifications. That data is stored only on the author's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
